- [02-refund-spends-held.patch](02-refund-spends-held.patch) — stage-4.md: “It moves existing money from the receiver's **available** funds, or fails 409 `insufficient_funds`, atomically.” Behaviour change: Checks a refund against total funds by treating all of the receiver's holds as spendable reserves; refunds can consume money reserved for other authorizations.
- [03-batch-offset-spelling.patch](03-batch-offset-spelling.patch) — stage-4.md: “Members of one settlement must have identical effective instants (offset spellings may differ), else 422 `validation_failed`.” Behaviour change: Compares timestamp strings, rejecting settlement corrections whose different offset spellings represent the same instant.
- [04-batch-individual-funds.patch](04-batch-individual-funds.patch) — stage-4.md: “Affordability is determined by the combined effect of all proposed revisions.” Behaviour change: Prechecks each revision in isolation, rejecting affordable batches when one correction's credit funds another correction's debit.
- [05-snapshot-future-cutoff.patch](05-snapshot-future-cutoff.patch) — stage-3.md: “It freezes the caller's selected revisions, window, balances, entries and default `to` at that read.” Behaviour change: Stores a future supplied `known_at` without clamping it to the read; later writes recorded before that future cutoff change saved statement pages.
- [06-hold-closure-knowledge.patch](06-hold-closure-knowledge.patch) — stage-3.md: “Events other than clock expiry are known at their server-assigned event time.” Behaviour change: Applies a later void or final-capture closure even when `known_at` predates it, releasing the historical hold before that closure was known.
- [07-overdraft-hold-boundaries.patch](07-overdraft-hold-boundaries.patch) — stage-3.md: “A correction is rejected with 409 `historical_overdraft` if it makes either total or available negative at any past effective/event boundary, under the latest known revisions.” Behaviour change: Omits hold lifecycle boundaries from overdraft checks, allowing a correction that overdraws available funds only during an intervening held interval.
- [08-statement-inclusive-end.patch](08-statement-inclusive-end.patch) — stage-3.md: “Returns the payments the caller sent or received in the half-open window `[from, to)`, **oldest first**, each with the caller's balance immediately after it”.
