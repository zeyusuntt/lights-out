## Finding V4-1 — a batch skips an affordable research run and leaves a field unknown

Sentences:
- Stage 4 Batches: "When the budget would be exceeded by a further call, the batch makes no more provider calls, and its remaining builds complete with the pages and research claims they have obtained".
- §8 / RULING R1-2: each field is known exactly when the evidence supports a value.
- Dispatch: "a saving that leaves out evidence a field needs is a defect".

Case:
- tarnwick-labs.test's founding year is supported only through the nimble research claim (its registry page is not searchable).
- An unbatched build of it spends 44 units and gets founded_year 1987.
- After a reset, `POST /batches {"domains": ["tarnwick-labs.test"], "budget_units": 49}`, a budget that covers that whole build, ends ready having spent 16 units with founded_year unknown. No call was refused by the budget.

Cause (code read): research.py `_research` skips the research run unless the shared budget affords RESEARCH_UNITS + PAGE_ROOM (25 + 30 = 55). With 49 units remaining, research (25) plus every page this build needs (19) fit, but the run is skipped and the evidence it alone reaches is lost. No sentence lets a batch drop an affordable call. R4-6 latches only on a call that cannot be reserved.

Reproduce: `run.sh <clone>/stage-4 -k post_seal_batch_budget` (seal sha256:438bf8f9…). Output: (budget 49, spent 16, founded_year unknown) vs 1987 unbatched.

Fix direction (no test source): decide research by whether it can be reserved, or plan the build's total cost against the remainder, rather than a fixed 30-unit page room. Keep R4-6's latch at reservation time.
